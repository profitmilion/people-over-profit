# People Over Profit (POP33)


## 🌍 Vision

We aim to build something that goes beyond traditional lotteries and crowdfunding.  
POP33 introduces a new hybrid model that offers participants **higher odds and better ROI** through transparent blockchain mechanics, fair tokenomics, and shared participation.

This is not just another lottery or funding system — it’s an **evolution**.  
The first of a new generation of models that will redefine how people take part in collective projects, wealth creation, and Web3 opportunities.

POP33 also carries a strong **educational mission** — helping people understand how participation in decentralized systems, community profits, and fair token distribution can empower individuals instead of intermediaries.  
It teaches shared ownership and responsibility in a global digital economy.

Our long-term goal is to create a framework that becomes a new standard for fairness and sustainability —  
**an innovative successor to both crowdfunding and lotteries.**

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Join:** Participants enter open crowdfunding cycles.  
2. **Pool closes:** When a cycle reaches its cap, it automatically locks.  
3. **Random draw:** The system fairly selects winners using transparent logic.  
4. **Redistribution:** A portion of funds supports new users or next rounds.  
5. **Loyalty reward:** Auto-HODL and “Last become first” mechanisms ensure continuous fairness and community growth.

## 🪙 Tokenomics (Draft v0.1)

At this stage, tokenomics is an evolving concept and may change as we test, simulate and discuss it with the community.  
Nothing in this section should be seen as a fixed or final economic model.

### Entry example: 33 USDC

One possible draft model:

- Each entry to the POP33 lottery-crowdfunding cycle could be 33 USDC.  
- From each 33 USDC, a small meta-fee of 3 USDC is split as follows:
  - 1 USDC goes to a special pool for monthly draws or benefits for people outside the active ticket holders (external support, subscriptions, etc.).
  - 1 USDC goes to development, maintenance and long-term sustainability of the project (dev, infra, audits, improvements).
  - 1 USDC is split into two parts:
    - 0.50 USDC to a long-term HODL or reserve pool,
    - 0.50 USDC used to buy PMN on Base, managed for ecosystem purposes (not personal hoarding).

This is an experimental design and will be refined.  
Percentages, destinations and exact mechanics can and will change as we learn and test.

### PMN Token – Core Idea (subject to change)

The long-term intention is that the **PMN Token (Profit Milion)** acts as the core token of the ecosystem.  
The current high-level idea is that:

- PMN may be used for governance (DAO voting and high-level decisions).  
- PMN may be strengthened by a portion of ecosystem flows (like the 0.50 USDC buy from entries), creating transparent on-chain demand.  
- PMN may be used in future liquidity pairs and reward mechanisms, once the model is tested and community approved.

All of the above is under active research and will be modified, corrected or simplified whenever we detect risks, flaws or better solutions.

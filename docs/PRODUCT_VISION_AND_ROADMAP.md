# POP33 Product Vision and Roadmap

## Document status and interpretation rule

This document describes the longer-term vision, possible directions, ideas,
and open product questions for POP33. It is a vision and roadmap document, not
a technical specification.

> **Important:** Anything marked `FUTURE`, `IDEA`, `TO DECIDE`, `PROPOSED`, or
> similar is not an approved implementation requirement, technical
> specification, financial promise, launch plan, schedule, or delivery
> commitment.

Current approved rules remain in `docs/BUSINESS_RULES.md` and
`docs/BASIC_V1_SPEC.md`. The current product model and implementation scope are
summarized in `docs/PRODUCT.md` and `docs/STATUS.md`. Nothing in this roadmap
changes those sources by itself.

## 1. CURRENT / APPROVED PRODUCT

POP33 is under active development. The approved Basic V1 model is a transparent
pool-based participation flow with positions, automatic pool assignment,
sequential draws, winner claims, and an on-chain results/archive experience.
Its detailed rules belong in the approved product and business documents, not
in this roadmap.

The current public implementation is the Pilot 10 alpha on Base Sepolia. It is
available through a branch Vercel Preview for testing. It is not Vercel
Production, a mainnet product, or evidence that the future ideas below have
been approved.

Current guiding principles include transparency, fairness, accessibility,
community benefit, education, and equal opportunity. Any new mechanism must be
reviewed before it becomes part of the approved product model.

## 2. CORE VISION

### People Over Profit ecosystem

The long-term vision is for POP33 to become a hybrid community ecosystem built
around the **People Over Profit** principle. It should not be understood merely
as a lottery. The wider concept may combine:

- helping other people and creating positive social impact;
- community participation, cooperation, and collective building;
- transparent on-chain mechanisms;
- an engaging interactive or game-like experience;
- financial, economic, and blockchain education;
- simple explanations of pools, wallets, transactions, smart contracts, and
  token economics;
- education about PMN and a possible future economy built around PMN;
- community building; and
- mechanisms intended to become economically sustainable as the ecosystem
  grows.

Economic sustainability is an aspiration to investigate, not a promise of
profit, return, value, or future performance.

### Community-funding concept

The founder currently thinks about POP33 partly as a new or hybrid form of
community funding or crowdfunding-like participation. This is product-concept
language only. POP33 is **not claimed here to legally qualify as
crowdfunding**. Legal and compliance classification must be completed before
such language is used for Production.

### Social mission

The central long-term idea is broader than winning or individual financial
benefit. A future POP33 ecosystem should encourage:

- helping other people;
- cooperation and community;
- transparency;
- building something collectively; and
- People Over Profit values.

`FUTURE / TO DECIDE`: A charitable or social-impact mechanism may become part
of the ecosystem. Its legal form, organization, governance, beneficiaries,
funding, economics, reporting, and relationship to POP33 are unresolved. This
document does not create or assume a charity structure.

### Education as a possible product pillar

`FUTURE`: Education may become a major part of the product and its
communication. POP33 could explain, in simple language:

- personal-finance concepts and basic economics;
- blockchain, wallets, transactions, and smart contracts;
- pool and draw mechanics;
- token economics and PMN;
- risks, limitations, and uncertainty; and
- the difference between transparent mechanics and promises of profit.

The educational layer should help people understand mechanisms. It must not
promise profit or present general educational material as personal financial,
legal, or investment advice. Its exact scope and review process are
`TO DECIDE`.

### Interactive and game-like experience

`FUTURE`: POP33 may develop into a more engaging interactive or game-like
product. Game mechanics should support participation, education, clarity, and
community. They should not hide financial mechanics, costs, risks, or on-chain
actions.

Exact gamification features, rewards, progression, eligibility, and safety
boundaries are `TO DECIDE` and are not approved for implementation.

## 3. FUTURE DIRECTION

### Multi-level POP33 concept

`PROPOSED / FUTURE / NOT APPROVED FOR IMPLEMENTATION`

POP33 could eventually support multiple participation levels inside one POP33
ecosystem and application:

```text
POP33 application
-> Level 1
-> Level 2
-> Level 3
-> future levels
```

Different levels could potentially use different entry amounts, pool sizes,
pool or prize economics, and separate smart contracts or contract instances.
Those possibilities do not select an architecture or approve any parameters.

No final entry price, pool size, economic model, contract boundary, routing
rule, or smart-contract architecture is defined here. All of them are
`TO DECIDE` and would require separate product, economic, legal, security, and
technical review.

A guiding design principle is that a new level does not necessarily replace an
earlier level. `FUTURE / TO DECIDE`: A small or low-value level could remain
available permanently after higher-value levels are introduced, so people who
prefer smaller participation amounts would not be forced to move to a higher
level. Permanent availability is not an approved Production requirement and
would still depend on later evidence, safety review, legal classification, and
explicit approval.

### Shared-economy question

`TO DECIDE`: Should different POP33 levels have completely independent
economics, or should every level contribute proportionally to a shared POP33
ecosystem?

Possible destinations to analyze include:

- pool or prize funding;
- PMN DCA/HODL mechanisms;
- free participation or free draws;
- development and operations;
- a treasury;
- social or charitable impact; and
- other future ecosystem functions.

These are categories for analysis only. This document assigns no percentages,
allocations, fees, entitlements, or financial commitments. Old percentages or
draft allocations must not be treated as approved rules. The entire economic
split is `TO DECIDE`.

The guiding idea is that each major new feature or level should ideally
strengthen the wider POP33 ecosystem economically or socially instead of
existing as an isolated feature. How that principle would be measured or
implemented is also `TO DECIDE`.

### Possible mainnet evolution

`FUTURE / TO DECIDE`: One possible evolution path is to begin mainnet operation
at a deliberately small participation level and add other levels only after
real-world validation.

This is not an approved deployment plan, launch sequence, schedule, or promise.
Mainnet parameters, legal and compliance gates, infrastructure, security
requirements, economic validation, and release criteria remain `TO DECIDE`.

### PMN and a possible future economy

`FUTURE / TO DECIDE`: PMN education and a future economy built around PMN may
become part of POP33. PMN utility, issuance, rights, governance, token
economics, DCA/HODL mechanisms, compliance, and relationship to other POP33
levels are not approved or specified here.

### Community, builders, and public project development

`FUTURE / IDEA / TO DECIDE / NOT APPROVED FOR IMPLEMENTATION`

POP33 may eventually show not only how many people participate in the product,
but also how many people help build the wider ecosystem. The project should be
honest about its current size: if there is one active founder or builder, it
should not imply that a larger team already exists.

A future visible counter could potentially distinguish categories such as:

- builders;
- contributors;
- public testers; and
- community participants.

These are example labels, not current counters or formal roles. Their names,
definitions, counting rules, verification, and boundaries are `TO DECIDE`.

Contribution would not have to mean programming. People could potentially help
through product development, entrepreneurship, business, smart contracts or
engineering, testing, design, security, economics, education, content,
communication, community building, translations, legal or compliance
knowledge, and other useful skills. This list does not create a formal role,
employment, reward, ownership, or governance structure.

`FUTURE / TO DECIDE`: A POP33 community or activity feed could eventually
surface meaningful public project activity, for example:

- new contributors;
- testing milestones;
- completed development milestones;
- new educational material;
- community achievements; and
- relevant public on-chain lifecycle events.

Exact event types, data sources, automation, publishing rules, accuracy checks,
moderation, and correction or removal processes remain `TO DECIDE`. No
automatic public-posting rule is approved.

Farcaster may become one distribution or community layer for this activity.
No POP33 Farcaster group, channel, activity-feed integration, or posting
automation is claimed to exist here. The exact Farcaster community architecture
is `TO DECIDE` and requires separate research and approval.

Contributor identity must not be assumed to be public. Displaying a person's
name, profile, wallet, role, or contribution history would require an
appropriate consent and privacy model. Identity fields, consent, withdrawal of
consent, verification, retention, and public visibility are `TO DECIDE`.

The guiding principle is:

> POP33 can show not only how many people participate in the product, but also
> how many people help build the ecosystem.

This concept does not approve counters, database architecture, Farcaster APIs,
contributor or token rewards, governance rights, salaries, ownership rights, or
automatic public posting.

## 4. FUTURE IDEAS / MILESTONES

The following are possible areas of exploration, not an ordered delivery plan
and not commitments:

1. Continue validating the approved pool lifecycle, user safety, and clear
   public explanations.
2. Explore an educational layer for finance, economics, blockchain, wallets,
   smart contracts, pool mechanics, token economics, PMN, risks, and
   limitations.
3. Research interactive and game-like experiences that improve understanding
   without hiding financial or technical mechanics.
4. Define options for community and social-impact participation without
   assuming a legal or organizational structure.
5. Analyze the multi-level concept, including whether successful lower levels
   should remain available when higher levels are introduced.
6. Compare independent level economics with a shared ecosystem model, without
   assigning percentages or allocations.
7. Evaluate whether and how PMN, DCA/HODL, free participation, operations,
   treasury, and social-impact categories could fit together.
8. Subject Production-facing language, economics, and operating models to
   legal, compliance, security, and financial review before approval.

Each area requires its own evidence and explicit decision before it can move
into the approved product documents or an implementation plan.

## 5. TO DECIDE

The following matters remain explicitly unresolved:

- legal classification of POP33 and whether any crowdfunding-related language
  is appropriate;
- legal, organizational, governance, funding, reporting, and economic model
  for any social or charitable mechanism;
- the scope, review process, and product form of the educational layer;
- exact game mechanics, rewards, progression, eligibility, and safeguards;
- whether POP33 should use multiple participation levels;
- entry amounts, pool sizes, prize or pool economics, routing, and lifecycle
  differences for any future level;
- whether levels use separate contracts, contract instances, or another
  technical architecture;
- whether level economics are independent or contribute to one shared
  ecosystem;
- every allocation, fee, percentage, entitlement, and destination within a
  future economic split;
- PMN utility, token economics, governance, DCA/HODL mechanics, and compliance;
- the role and economics of free participation or free draws;
- development, operations, treasury, and other ecosystem funding;
- mainnet scope, sequencing, parameters, infrastructure, validation, security,
  compliance, and release criteria; and
- which vision pillars should appear in Production marketing and when.

## 6. NOT YET APPROVED FOR IMPLEMENTATION

The following are vision concepts only and must not be treated as engineering
requirements:

- a multi-level POP33 product;
- any new entry amount, pool size, prize model, or economic allocation;
- shared economics across levels;
- a PMN economy, PMN utility, or DCA/HODL mechanism;
- free participation or free draws;
- a charitable or social-impact organization or funding mechanism;
- new gamification systems;
- a specific education product;
- a specific future smart-contract architecture; and
- a staged small-level mainnet launch.

Before implementation, an idea must be separately analyzed, explicitly
approved, moved into the appropriate product/business specification, and
reviewed for legal, compliance, security, economic, and technical impact.

## Communication and marketing boundary

Future pillars may later influence the landing page, FAQ, Farcaster
communication, tester materials, website, educational content, and community
materials.

Production-facing language involving finance, prizes, tokens, crowdfunding,
charity, returns, or profit requires legal and compliance review before use.
Communication must distinguish current functionality from future vision and
must not turn an idea into a promise.

## Product-development principles

- New functionality should ideally strengthen the existing POP33 ecosystem
  rather than merely add disconnected features.
- A successful existing level does not have to be removed when a new level is
  introduced.
- Transparency, understandable mechanics, risks, and limitations should remain
  visible as the product evolves.
- A future idea becomes part of the product only after an explicit decision;
  inclusion in this roadmap is not approval.

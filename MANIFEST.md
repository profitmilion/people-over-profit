# People Over Profit (POP33) Manifest

> "The world does not need more corporations.  
> It needs more people who care — not about themselves, but about others."  
> — Profit Milion

---

## 🌍 Our Purpose

People Over Profit (POP33) is more than a project.  
It is a movement that challenges systems built on greed, inequality and manipulation.  
We believe that technology — especially blockchain — can serve humanity, not enslave it.

POP33 is our first step toward that goal:  
a transparent, fair and automated economy where *everyone* can participate, win and grow — not just the few.

---

## 💡 Our Principles

### 1️⃣ Fairness over privilege  
Every person deserves an equal chance to participate and succeed.  
Rules must be public, transparent and shared — not hidden behind privilege.

### 2️⃣ Community over hierarchy  
Real power belongs to people — not founders, whales or corporations.  
POP33 will evolve into a decentralized community where decisions come from the bottom up, not the top down.

### 3️⃣ Transparency over manipulation  
All transactions, token flows and decisions must remain verifiable and open.  
There are no hidden wallets, private sales or secret investors.  
Trust is built through math, not promises.

### 4️⃣ Learning over speculation  
Every project under the People Over Profit ecosystem has an educational purpose.  
We want people to understand Web3 — not gamble with it.  
Knowledge and awareness are the foundation of independence.

### 5️⃣ Purpose over profit  
Money itself is not evil — greed is.  
But the real problem begins where the free market loses balance,  
and individualism and social inequality spiral out of control.  
We are building a system where value flows back to people,  
and community and cooperation become the new currency of trust.  
True wealth is measured by impact, not accumulation.

### 6️⃣ The last become the first  
Those who stay loyal, who believe in fairness,  
who share and support others —  
they will always stand first in our system.

## 🪙 The POP33 Vision

POP33 combines the logic of crowdfunding and lotteries in a new form —  
a **hybrid economy** designed to give more people real chances of benefit.  
It is an evolving experiment in fairness and collective empowerment,  
not a speculative token game.

Every contribution, ticket or donation supports the next participant,  
and every cycle redistributes hope, trust and opportunity.

---

## 💠 The Role of PMN

The **PMN Token (Profit Milion)** is the heart of our ecosystem —  
not as a speculative asset, but as a symbol of unity and participation.

PMN connects all projects within the People Over Profit movement.  
It represents contribution, loyalty and shared responsibility.  
It belongs to everyone who helps, builds or supports — not to one person.

## ⚖️ Moral Foundation

We reject greed, secrecy and elitism.  
We believe in cooperation, equality and human dignity.  
Every person has the right to participate in a system that treats them as equal —  
and the duty to act with integrity toward others.

Technology without morality is merely a tool of control.  
POP33 exists to restore the human spirit to the digital economy  
and to increase fairness, inclusion and opportunity  
compared to the large centralized systems that dominate today’s markets.
